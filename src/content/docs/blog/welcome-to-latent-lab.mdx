Running AI on your own infrastructure used to mean stitching together GPU drivers, serving engines, gateways, and auth. Nutanix Enterprise AI packages that into a platform, but a platform is only as useful as people's ability to use it.

That’s why we built Latent Lab: one place to go from *"what's a token?"* to *"how do I run a multi-model platform for 5,000 developers?"*

## What you'll find here

- **Concepts** that explain the why, briefly.
- **Tutorials** that get you to a working result.
- **Recipes** for wiring NAI into the tools your teams already love.
- **Demos** you can run and present.

## What's next

We're working on GPU sizing guides, an agent-building track, and a library of integration recipes. Have a request? See [how to contribute](/contribute/content-guide/).
