## The story

> *"Our policies, runbooks, and contracts are spread across thousands of documents. People can't find answers, and we can't send that data to a public AI service."*

This demo shows a private assistant that answers in plain language, cites its sources, and keeps every byte on your infrastructure.

## Talking points

- **Data sovereignty:** prompts, documents, and embeddings never leave the cluster.
- **Cost predictability:** GPU capacity you own, not per-token billing.
- **Swap models freely:** endpoints are OpenAI-compatible, so upgrading the model is a config change.
